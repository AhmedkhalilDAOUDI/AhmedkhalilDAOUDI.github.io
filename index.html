01 / The story

Jack of all trades.

I’m Ahmed Khalil Daoudi. My path runs from applied mathematics and finance through industrial quality to data and AI. Each discipline taught me a different way to ask whether a system really works.

QHSE gave me a practical respect for traceability, risk, and process. Data engineering taught me to make information reliable and available. Data science and AI engineering let me turn that foundation into models, retrieval systems, and useful software.

I’m interested in the whole chain: the question, the data, the method, the result, and the evidence that connects them. The projects below show that range in practice.
